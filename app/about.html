Sift

Tasks, day plans, thoughts, lists, recipes, contacts, scans and contracts, all in one place. Sift is free, runs in your browser and keeps your things on your own device.

Open Sift

Google Calendar in the Day Planner

You can show your Google Calendar beside your day plan, so you can plan around your appointments and copy an event into your plan. Sift only reads the events of the calendars you choose and never changes them. The events go straight from Google to your browser and stay on that device.

Privacy

No Sift account, no advertising, no analytics, no tracking. Read the privacy policy.
