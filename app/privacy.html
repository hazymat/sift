Sift privacy policy

Last updated 1 October 2026

Sift is a free app for tasks, day plans, notes, lists and recipes. It runs in your browser at hazymat.github.io/sift. There is no Sift account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

Your own data

Everything you put into Sift is kept in your browser's storage on your own device. It only leaves your device if you turn on sync and enter the address of a sync server. Synced records and files are encrypted on your device with a key the server never receives, so the server cannot read them.

Google Calendar

If you press Connect Google Calendar (Day Planner, Bring items in), Sift asks Google for permission to read your calendar events and the names of your calendars. Sift never changes, adds or deletes anything in your calendars.

  • What is read: the list of your calendars (name and colour), so you can choose which ones show; and, for the days you look at and the calendars you chose (your main calendar to start with), each event's name, start and end time, place, description and link.
  • How it is used: only to show your events with your day plan, and to let you copy an event into your plan when you ask.
  • Where it goes: straight from Google to your browser. It is kept on that device only, is never sent to a Sift server, is never synced, and is never shared with anyone.
  • How long it is kept: until you fetch those days again or disconnect. Pressing Disconnect under Google Calendar in Bring items in deletes the calendar data Sift kept on the device and cancels Sift's permission with Google. You can also remove Sift's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Sift's permission from Google lasts about an hour at a time and is kept only in the open browser tab.

Sift's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is not used for advertising, is not sold, is not read by people, and is not used to train AI or machine learning models.

Cookies

Sift sets no cookies of its own. Google's sign-in window is run by Google under Google's privacy policy.

Changes and questions

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date. Questions can be asked at github.com/hazymat/sift/issues.
