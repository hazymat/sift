# UI consistency

How every area of Sift shows its controls, to find where areas do the same job in different ways. All new UI follows the **standards** below. Where there's no standard yet, copy what most areas already do and add the choice here. The same text is GitHub issue #284; keep the two in sync.

Audited at 1.54.09 (29 September 2026), updated for 1.60.11.

## Standards to follow

Every version that changes something on screen adds a What's new entry with a Show me pointing at it (`app/js/whatsnew.js`, see "What's new" in `docs/working-notes.md`).

**Buttons and menus**
- **+ New [thing]** is a pill at the top left of the page. Scans' "Scan" button is the one exception (it takes a photo), with "From photos" beside it for photos already taken.
- **⋯ menus** hold only rarely used things (Show Archive, Show Bin, import, export, example data). Primary actions never go in one. ⋯ menus open below the top bar and sticky bars and scroll inside if long (1.55.03).
- **Green means open or close** (More, More (full), ✓ Close, a note's ⤢ and its Done), and a green button with a key shows it (hidden on touch screens) (1.55.04, 1.55.14). Shared rule in `app.css` (search `.close-top, .md-full`).
- **Printing** (1.60.96): 🖨 Print… is the last item of a Share menu, under a line (in Advanced's Share sheet, a 🖨 Print… button). It opens the one Print screen (`openPrint` in `print.js`): ‹ Back Esc top left, choices on the left, a live preview on the right (the page that prints), 🖨 Print Ctrl+P under the choices. Every printout has Look (Colour, or Black and white: lines only, no blocks of colour, photos in grey) and Paper (A5, A4, A3, Letter); each kind of thing keeps its own choices on the device. No Print buttons outside Share menus. An area's Share prints what the page shows as a list (only the chosen rows when some are chosen); an opened thing with a printout of its own sets it in `printers` (`share.js`), as a case does (1.60.98). Every Share menu copies as plain text, rich text, WhatsApp and Markdown. Sharing with people is the first item of the Share menu (Day Planner, an open list or project: 👥 Share with someone…), never a pill of its own (1.60.99). Things to print that are pictures (a scan) swap Look for their own tone choice (Colour, Grey, Black and white) with Contrast and Lightness; an opened thing whose words aren't rows sets `sharers` (`share.js`) so the copies share its words (1.61.00). More printouts of one thing go after Print… in the same menu (Batch Book: 🏷 Print labels…, 1.61.01). Every opened thing has the Share pill in its top bar; a thing whose printout is its details uses `printRecord` (`print.js`), as a contact and a contract do (1.61.03). Under Print… every Share menu has 🖼 Share as picture… and 📄 Share as PDF…: the same Print screen (`shareNext` in `print.js`), ending in Share (`printshare.js`), the printout as a file for the share sheet (1.61.08). Printouts with photos pass `photos: true` for the Photo size choice and size pictures by `var(--ps)`.
- **The picture and file viewer** (`view()` in `attachments.js`, used everywhere a photo or file opens) has round glass buttons: ✕ top right, Share top left (the device's share sheet with the file itself, or Save a copy where files can't be shared), ‹ › at the sides (at the bottom on phones) (1.60.75).
- **Switching between parts of an area** (Tasks | Projects, Recipes | Batches) is always visible on the page, never in a menu.
- **Filter bars and section tabs** are underlined tabs in one row under the page's top buttons, the shared `.dump-filter-row` / `.dump-filter` (Brain Dump, Tasks, Find Things' life areas, Batch Book's books, Contacts' Recent | Directory | Cases (coloured with an emoji each, as Batch Book's books, 1.60.91), Lists' Lists | Templates, Scans, Contracts, Tidied's areas) (1.58.10, 1.59.01). Never plain-text headings to split a page into parts.

**Keys**
- **Shift+Enter** goes one step further: name, then quick edit with its note, then the full panel. Lists and the Day Planner's tasks (not its schedule) have no quick stage and go straight to the panel (1.55.05, 1.58.06, 1.58.09).
- **↑ / ↓** move editing between rows edited in place (Tasks, Day Planner, an open list, a box's things); **Shift+↑ / ↓** select. The blue browsing box is only for cards and grids (1.55.07). Only from the row's own name: never from Add a comment or another field in a row, and not at all in Advanced, where tasks are chosen only from the ⠿ (with text typed it selects to the start or end of the line; Esc clears a choice, 1.60.78).
- **Shift+Alt+↑ / ↓** in a row's name or note moves the row up or down past its neighbour, carrying its sub-rows; a sub-row stays inside its parent ("Already the first sub-task" at the end). Top-level rows cross a project's milestone and person headings, as a drag does, but nothing else (never Advanced's Done heading). The moved row (and its sub-rows) pulses yellow once where it lands (`MOVED` in `flash.js`, the Show me yellow, 1.60.84). The cursor stays in the same field at the same place, and the new order saves as a drag's does, with Undo. Tasks, an open list, a box's things and Advanced through `listkit.js` (`stepRow`, `refocus` for views whose note editor opens on its own); the Day Planner's Tasks in `views/planner.js`. Not from Add a comment (`data-no-move`), not on cards or grids, not in the schedule (its order is the time) (1.60.82).
- **Ticking off** (one tick, or Done / Tick on a selection bar) plays the same animation everywhere: `tickWave` then `fadeFold` in `tickwave.js` (tick springs with a ring, letters hop, light passes, line drawn, then fade and fold if the row is leaving). Tasks, Projects, Day Planner, Lists (1.56.02).
- **Key labels on buttons** use `keys()` from `keys.js` (one box per key). A button showing a key centres its text, icon and keys as boxes (one rule in `app.css`), so they sit in the middle on every platform, Windows included (1.60.02).
- **Esc closes a full panel** (✓ Close Esc) wherever the focus is, unless a sheet or pill menu is open on top (Day Planner 1.60.04).
- **Alt+Enter opens a note full screen** wherever its ⤢ shows Alt Enter, whatever has the focus (a task's name, a button, nothing); the hint shows only where the key does that. A row's name with no panel open: Alt+Enter opens the panel, and no note shows the hint. One rule for every area (`altTarget` in `richtext.js`, 1.60.08).
- **Selection bars**: ✕ (clear, Esc) first, then the count, then the actions; a new selection always shows the bar from its start (1.60.49). A Archive, D (or Delete) Delete, Ctrl+Enter Done or Tick, each key shown on its button. Built into `listkit.js` (1.55.13). A button that opens more (Move ▸, Move to ▸, Status ▸) shares one shaded pill with what it opens while open, and on a narrow bar the bar slides along to show them (1.60.30).
- **Things floating at the bottom** stack, never overlap: the selection bar lowest, then the "new version" banner, then toasts (Undo, Moved, Saved). One set of CSS variables (`--sel-lift`, `--upd-lift` in `app.css`) does it for every area (1.58.12).
- **View case** (1.60.62, 1.60.63): one task on a screen of its own (`casepage.js`). In Advanced a View case chip sits beside each name (where More was; the full panel is ⋯ or Shift+Enter); a task's full panel (Tasks, the Day Planner, Advanced) also has View case. Leaving is only "‹ Back Esc" top left, back to the view it came from; ✓ Mark case done / Reopen case sits under the status, never beside Back. Order: name, note, Add a comment and 📎, status; history (newest first); then the other details (1.60.65). Esc steps out of the comment line first; nothing underneath takes that Esc. It uses Advanced's ticket look (1.60.68): `.adv-status`, `.adv-stat` tiles, `.adv-card` with `.adv-card-h`, `.adv-group` day bars.
- **Logging something** (a contact's Log, a case's New entry) is one box (`.log-box`): kind and who contacted whom, then what happened, then files waiting to go on it, then When with Now, 📎 Attach and Log it on the right; on a phone each is a row of its own. Files belong to the entry (an `interactions` or `case_notes` record), shown under it in the Timeline (1.60.88). Nothing else sits right under the box: a case's + Task for this case is bottom left, as a contact's + Task with this contact. A case's kinds end with ✎ Note (a plain note, no one contacted); there is no separate Add note. When follows the clock until it's changed by hand (Now puts it back). Each saved entry has a faint ✎ that opens it in place: When, kind, words, Save Enter, Cancel Esc, Delete (1.60.85).
- **A case's page** (Contacts and Cases, 1.60.92): no area header while a contact or case is open; top row "‹ Cases Esc" left, Share and ⋯ right (⋯: edit references and people, add a task, archive, delete). Every control on it is one size (`.kc-btn`: 44px high, 14px corners, glass; round ones 44px circles). A hero card with the status colour down its left edge (as a project's colour): "📁 Case · opened ..." above the name as a heading, the summary under it (both edited in place), a big status pill that opens Open / Waiting / Closed (no status pills anywhere else), three figures (days open, entries, since last), then the references (tap copies) and people as chips with ✎ Edit. Then New entry, the one raised card: kinds as big emoji tiles, Received | Sent, what happened (Ctrl+Enter logs), and one bar: When (Now, following the clock; tap to pick a date; ↺ back to now), 📷, 📎, Log it. Then Photos and documents as big square tiles with + first, then the Timeline (a rail with each event's emoji in a circle, lined up, ringed blue for received and amber for sent, each event a callout starting with its date, month bars), then + Task, Archive, Delete. Entries read "Letter received", "Call made" and so on.
- **Files on a record** use `attachments.js` everywhere (thumbnails, 📎 Attach, drop, the shared viewer). A case has them under Files, above its Timeline, each also shown in the Timeline when it was attached (1.60.85).
- **Esc on an opened page**: the first Esc leaves the field (keeping what's typed; an Add items box adds it), the next goes back. The back button shows Esc when nothing is being typed (1.55.15).

- **Full-screen screens** (the Print screen, Batch Book's Follow along) have "‹ Back Esc" top left and cover everything, the area's header included; Esc or Back leaves (1.61.12). Follow along's steps are ticked off with a green ✓ Done, next pill at the bottom right of the step in the middle (Enter), which shows a big green ✓ on the step's left and rolls on; ↺ Not done takes it back (1.61.15). Ending one (✓ Finished cooking) never asks: Undo in the message brings it back (1.61.14).
- **Cards' ⋯ menus** open over everything on the page, sticky bars included (1.61.14). Batch Book's cards keep only ☆ and ⋯ under them; ▶ Follow along and 🧪 Make lead the ⋯ menu (1.61.14).
- **Timers** (`timers.js`, 1.61.12) are pills: the time big, the name small under it, ⏸ / ▶ beside it, ✕ once a countdown has rung (red, pulsing, beeping until stopped); tapping the time opens a vertical list under it (its time and name, then +5, +1, −1, −5 min, which keep it open, ☑ Choose several, Rename, Remove with Undo, never a confirm) (1.61.13). Press and hold a timer to choose several: the same selection bar as lists (✕ Esc, count, All, ±5 / ±1 min, Pause, Carry on, Remove), above Follow along too. A step's Start timer starts one timer per step; tapping again flashes the one going. They sit in Follow along's top bar and, on every other page, stacked at the bottom right above the bar of areas (`#timer-dock`), lifting with the selection bar and the update banner like toasts.
**Rows, cards and pages**
- **Filter rows and the arrow keys**: every filter row (the underlined `dump-filter` tabs, or segmented tabs at a page's top) goes in `bars` in `browse.js`'s table, so ↓ stops on it between the search box and the items, as in Brain Dump. Rows edited in place go in `rows`, so Esc out of one leaves it highlighted (1.61.04).
- **Choosing several cards**: things shown as cards or boxes (Find Things' boxes, Brain Dump notes, Batch Book recipes, Contacts, the Lists page's lists and templates (1.60.00)) can be chosen by pressing and holding one, which brings up the selection bar, as in Batch Book; while any are chosen, a tap adds or removes one (Shift+tap: a run). Shift+arrows select while browsing. Esc or the bar's ✕ clears. Holding never starts text selection, and a chosen card shows the same accent ring everywhere (1.58.14). Built into `listkit.js` (`holdSelect`) and `browse.js` (1.56.00, 1.57.00).
- **A card's ⠿**: top left corner, as on Batch Book's recipe cards, with the card's first line moved right to make room. On a computer it shows on hover; on touch screens it is always there. Clicking it chooses the card. One shared CSS rule places and shows it on every card grid (search `.box-card, .c-card, .bb-card-li, .list-card-li` in `app.css`). Brain Dump notes have theirs at the start of the head row, the same spot (1.57.04).
- **Opening a card** zooms: the card grows into the opened page, and going back shrinks the page into its card (`zoom.js`: Find Things' boxes, the Lists page's lists, templates and shared lists, 1.60.00).
- **Managing categories, areas and groups** (Brain Dump's types, Find Things' life areas and groups) always uses the one manager sheet (`openManager` in `typesheet.js`): a pull-up list to add, rename, drag to reorder and remove, opened by the ⋯ at the end of the filter bar. No small pop-up menus for this. Removing one with things in it asks first and says what goes to the Bin with it (1.56.00, 1.57.03). Two levels (Find Things: life areas with their groups) are one sheet with the inner level indented, a short hint at the top, and the Tasks way of moving rows: hold and drag, drop onto a row, Tab / Shift+Tab, ⠿ to choose several (1.58.00).
- **Swiping sideways on an opened thing** (a project, list, box, contact, case, recipe, batch, scan or contract) goes back, either way, as its ‹ button does: a box goes back to the boxes, a project to Projects and then Tasks. Built once in `app.js` (`installSwipe`, any visible `#main button.back`); Tasks does it for projects through ← / → (1.57.05).
- **Adding to an opened thing** (a project's tasks, an open list, a box's contents): the add box is at the top, above what's in it, as Tasks. In a box it is one line in line with the things, with a faint cube where theirs are (no lined paper, no rounded box); in Tasks and Lists it follows 👁 Layout (a rounded box with Add Enter when lined paper is off). ↓ from it goes to the first row (1.57.06, 1.57.07).
- **A long opened thing keeps its top in view** as it scrolls: an open list's head and ticks bar, a box's back and search bar and its whole lid (label, where it lives, notes, photo), a project's head and progress, each with a glass backing once stuck (1.57.09, 1.58.02). A field that is usually empty (a box's Notes) shows as a small + Add pill until it has something.
- **Editing a row**: click anywhere on its line to start, anywhere outside to stop.
- **Sideways rows** (filter bars, tabs, pill rows) scroll with the mouse wheel; at the end the page scrolls. Built once (`installWheelRows` in `app.js`) (1.55.02). They keep where they were scrolled to when their page is drawn again, and the chosen tab is brought into view; a new sideways row joins `SIDEWAYS_ROWS` in `app.js` (`installRowKeep`) (1.60.90).
- **Search boxes** share one style with a ✕ to clear (1.55.11). Tasks, Projects, Lists and the Day Planner have none on purpose: the top bar search covers them.
- **Sticky top**: on an area's main page, the whole top (+ New pills, 👁, ⋯, search, filter bar) stays in view while scrolling, with a glass background once stuck (Batch Book, Find Things 1.58.01, Tasks, Projects and Contacts 1.58.03). Brain Dump keeps its New note box scrolling away and pins its search and types. A sticky bar's gap above it (`--stick-pad`) is always there, pulled up by as much, so only the glass changes when it sticks: nothing moves (1.61.06). A new sticky bar joins that rule in app.css and watches for sticking with its top plus its top padding.
- **Details tables** (a list shown like a file explorer's details view: Contacts and Cases' Cases, 1.60.94) use `detailtable.js`: drag a heading's right edge to resize (double-click: back to normal), click a heading to sort (again: the other way, dates newest first), and its ▾ sorts and filters like a spreadsheet (ticked values, Contains…, or a period for dates). Widths, sort and filters are a synced view choice (`choiceOf` / `setChoice` in `viewcog.js`), and a ✕ Clear filters pill shows on the filter bar while any are on. On phones the table scrolls sideways inside its box.
- **Sorting cards** is in 👁, a Sort choice above the rest, with Your order (dragging) first; dragging in a list sorted another way switches it to Your order quietly (Batch Book's recipes, per book, 1.61.11).
- **A list with more than one way to show it** (Cases: Details or Cards) has a "Show cases as" choice in 👁 in place of Spacing (1.60.94).
- **👁 Spacing on card grids** changes the cards' size: Tight is small cards (more on screen), Loose is big ones showing more of each (Find Things' boxes 1.58.01). Batch Book's cards: Tight is the picture and name only, smaller; Medium has the description but no tags or Made line; Loose shows everything; Make, ★ and ⋯ stay in every spacing (1.60.97).
- **Cards with a photo** keep one shape: the photo is cropped to its frame (square on recipe cards, 4:3 on scans) and never sets the card's height (1.55.17).
- **A thing's colour** shows the project way wherever it has one: a 4px coloured edge on its card and page head, and its progress bar in its colour, whatever the Look. Built on `--c` (1.55.08).
- **Dragging rows** is `listkit.js` + `sortable.js` everywhere rows are reordered (Tasks, Lists, Advanced day tasks): hold anywhere on a row to lift it, the tilt and wobble, rows sliding aside, and the dashed outline (`.drop-slot`) where it will land, in every list whether or not rows can be dropped onto each other (1.60.57). Never a drag of a view's own.
- **Rows of things inside an opened thing** (a recipe's batches) are the same rows as their own list (Batch Book's Batches): ⠿ to choose several, the same selection bar, and a red Delete pill (white words, as a swiped row's Delete) always at the right end of each row, Undo in the message (1.61.09, 1.61.10). The row is one glass box with its ⠿ inside on the left, as a task's in Tasks.
- **Deleting something with a linked copy** (a task and its Day Planner copies) asks Delete everywhere, Only in [where you are] or Cancel, wherever the delete is made (⋯, panel, Selections bar, swipe, emptied name). Taking a task out of a project shared with someone (delete everywhere, Make unique, Move, don't copy) always gets a second red sheet with Cancel focused (1.60.57).
- **Putting something into a project** (Move, 📁 Project pill, Brain Dump conversion, any picker) lists projects shared with you too, with 👥.
- **Who does a task**: a task's owner (👤 name, shared projects only) and Waiting on (⏳ name) are pills on the task's own line, in every spacing including tight, never squeezed out; nothing shows when nobody is set. Built once in `row()` in `views/tasks.js`, so every list of tasks (Now, Next, Later, All, Done, a project's page) has them (1.60.43). A shared project filters by person with the standard underlined filter bar under its name (Everyone, Mine, each person's, Nobody's), and its order and grouping are in the 👁 menu as "This project" buttons, like Look (1.60.45). In its quick edit the 👤 Who pill takes Energy's place, and its Selections bar has Assign to…, opening the people as a small list above the button, as Move ▸ Project… does (`.list-menu`, 1.60.50). Inside any project the bar has no Move ▸: tasks stay in their project, and a misfiled one moves from its panel (1.60.48).

## 3. Item level

Rows and cards inside an area. "Top right" means on the row's own first line, at the right.

| | Task (Tasks, project page) | Day Planner item | List item | Brain Dump note | Box item (Find Things) | Recipe card | Contact card | Scan / contract |
