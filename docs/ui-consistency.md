# UI consistency

How every area of Sift shows its controls, to find where areas do the same job in different ways. All new UI follows the **standards** below. Where there's no standard yet, copy what most areas already do and add the choice here. The same text is GitHub issue #284; keep the two in sync.

Audited at 1.54.09 (29 September 2026), updated for 1.60.11.

## Standards to follow

Every version that changes something on screen adds a What's new entry with a Show me pointing at it (`app/js/whatsnew.js`, see "What's new" in `docs/working-notes.md`).

**Buttons and menus**
- **+ New [thing]** is a pill at the top left of the page. Scans' "Scan" button is the one exception (it takes a photo), with "From photos" beside it for photos already taken.
- **⋯ menus** hold only rarely used things (Show Archive, Show Bin, import, export, example data). Primary actions never go in one. ⋯ menus open below the top bar and sticky bars and scroll inside if long (1.55.03).
- **Green means open or close** (More, More (full), ✓ Close, a note's ⤢ and its Done), and a green button with a key shows it (hidden on touch screens) (1.55.04, 1.55.14). Shared rule in `app.css` (search `.close-top, .md-full`).
- **Printing** (1.60.96): 🖨 Print… is the last item of a Share menu, under a line (in Advanced's Share sheet, a 🖨 Print… button). It opens the one Print screen (`openPrint` in `print.js`): ‹ Back Esc top left, choices on the left, a live preview on the right (the page that prints), 🖨 Print Ctrl+P under the choices. Every printout has Look (Colour, or Black and white: lines only, no blocks of colour, photos in grey) and Paper (A5, A4, A3, Letter); each kind of thing keeps its own choices on the device. No Print buttons outside Share menus. An area's Share prints what the page shows as a list (only the chosen rows when some are chosen); an opened thing with a printout of its own sets it in `printers` (`share.js`), as a case does (1.60.98). Every Share menu copies as plain text, rich text, WhatsApp and Markdown. Sharing with people is the first item of the Share menu (Day Planner, an open list or project: 👥 Share with someone…), never a pill of its own (1.60.99). Things to print that are pictures (a scan) swap Look for their own tone choice (Colour, Grey, Black and white) with Contrast and Lightness; an opened thing whose words aren't rows sets `sharers` (`share.js`) so the copies share its words (1.61.00). More printouts of one thing go after Print… in the same menu (Batch Book: 🏷 Print labels…, 1.61.01). Every opened thing has the Share pill in its top bar; a thing whose printout is its details uses `printRecord` (`print.js`), as a contact and a contract do (1.61.03). Under Print… every Share menu has 🖼 Share as picture… and 📄 Share as PDF…: the same Print screen (`shareNext` in `print.js`), ending in Share (`printshare.js`), the printout as a file for the share sheet (1.61.08). Printouts with photos pass `photos: true` for the Photo size choice and size pictures by `var(--ps)`.
- **Typing in someone to share with** (1.61.79): the share sheet's email box suggests people you've shared with before or who share with you (not you, not already on it), by `personName`, with the email beside them when two names match. One or two: 👤 chips under the box. More: a `.dd-menu` list under the box, in the sheet's flow (never cut off), narrowed as you type; ↑ ↓ move, Enter picks, Esc closes only the list. Picking fills the box and moves to Share.
- **Adding to a case's references and people** (1.62.37): one box (`factbox.js`), never a picker of contacts on its own. Focused and empty, it lists recent people; typing narrows them (name, text, number) and adds Add "…" as a reference and Add "…" as a new person, the likelier first (a phone number, email or words: person; anything else with a digit: reference). A `.dd-menu` in the page's flow; ↑ ↓ move, Enter adds and keeps the box for the next, Enter on empty or a second Esc is Done. A contact with no name is listed by `contactLabel` (the text or number it was made from), never "(no name)".
